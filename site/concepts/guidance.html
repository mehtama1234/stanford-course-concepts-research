Guidance

Steering generation toward a prompt, class, reward, or constraint.

What real-world problem is this about?

The problem is that pure generation may create plausible samples that do not match what the user asked for.

Why does this problem exist?

Generation needs both freedom and control: enough freedom to make rich outputs, enough constraint to satisfy the condition.

What is the mathematical idea underneath?

Guidance modifies the update direction during sampling so the path favors samples consistent with the condition.

Why is this concept important?

It makes image and video models controllable by text, labels, layouts, or preferences.

What breaks without it?

The model may produce realistic but irrelevant outputs.
