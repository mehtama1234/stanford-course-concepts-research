Pretraining

Large-scale practice before the model is asked to be useful in a specific way.

What real-world problem is this about?

The problem is that labeled examples are scarce, but raw text, images, actions, and videos are abundant.

Why does this problem exist?

A learner can absorb structure by being given a simple prediction game over huge data, even before people specify the final task.

What is the mathematical idea underneath?

Training reduces a loss, which is a number measuring how surprised or wrong the model was. Lower loss means the model assigns more probability to what actually happened.

Why is this concept important?

It creates broad reusable knowledge that later tuning can steer.

What breaks without it?

Every task would need its own large hand-labeled dataset, and models would learn narrow behavior from scratch.
