Policy

The rule an agent uses to choose what to do next.

What real-world problem is this about?

The problem is that the output is an action, and that action changes what happens afterward.

Why does this problem exist?

In a changing environment, the learner must connect what it can see now to a choice under uncertainty.

What is the mathematical idea underneath?

A policy is often written as pi(a|s): the chance of taking action a when the system is in situation s.

Why is this concept important?

It is the central object being learned in reinforcement learning and agentic LLM systems.

What breaks without it?

There is no explicit way to discuss or improve the system's behavior over time.
