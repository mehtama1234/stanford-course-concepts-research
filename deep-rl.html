Stanford CS224R Deep Reinforcement Learning

Deep RL Lecture Guide

This page reads the course lecture by lecture. Each section starts with the ordinary problem, then names the course idea only after the need for it is clear.

Course Spine

Deep RL studies a learner that acts, sees what changed, receives a score, and changes its future action rule. The hard parts are delay, missing data, noisy scores, unsafe trial and error, and tests that do not show the whole behavior.
