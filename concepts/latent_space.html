Latent Space

A compressed internal space where generation or prediction can happen more cheaply.

What real-world problem is this about?

The problem is that pixels and long sequences are expensive and full of surface detail.

Why does this problem exist?

If the model can work in a smaller space that preserves meaning, it can spend computation on structure instead of every raw detail.

What is the mathematical idea underneath?

An encoder maps data into a smaller vector space, and a decoder maps it back. The useful trick is preserving important variation while discarding nuisance detail.

Why is this concept important?

Latent spaces make large vision generation faster and connect images, text, and actions through shared coordinates.

What breaks without it?

Generation and planning may become too expensive at raw resolution.
