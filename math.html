One meta-move underneath: take a thing you cannot compute directly — the best choice, a sample from an impossible distribution, the value of a state, the gradient of a log-probability — and replace it with a differentiable surrogate you CAN push downhill. Softmax makes choice differentiable; the score makes sampling differentiable; the Bellman recursion makes long-horizon value computable; the policy gradient makes reward differentiable. The other half of the craft is the leash — KL penalties, baselines, staying inside the data — that stops the surrogate from being gamed. That is the whole subject.
