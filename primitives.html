Mathematical Primitives

The same small patterns recur across transformers, RL, diffusion, and vision.

Compression

Keep the parts that matter for the next decision and let go of the rest.

Why it exists: Inputs, memories, images, and action histories are too large to carry forward unchanged.

Concepts: tokenization, embeddings, attention, latent space

Assignment

Decide which piece of evidence should be connected to which cause, label, action, or memory.

Why it exists: Many causes can produce the same observation, so learning has to sort responsibility.

Concepts: attention, fine tuning, credit assignment, score matching

Credit

Decide which earlier choice deserves blame or praise for something that happened later.

Why it exists: Consequences arrive after chains of actions, not immediately after the action that mattered.

Concepts: reward, credit assignment, policy gradient, actor critic, q learning, rl for llms

Geometry

Arrange things so nearby points mean similar things and directions mean useful changes.

Why it exists: A machine needs a numerical space where comparison and movement can be computed.

Concepts: embeddings, positional encoding, score matching, flow matching, latent space

Search

Try to move through possible answers, actions, or images toward ones that satisfy a goal.

Why it exists: Hard problems rarely reveal the right answer in one step.

Concepts: reasoning traces, agents and tools, policy, q learning, model based rl, exploration, diffusion, flow matching, guidance

Uncertainty

Keep track of many possible futures or explanations instead of pretending there is only one.

Why it exists: Observations are incomplete, and the same present can lead to many futures.

Concepts: evaluation, policy, offline rl, model based rl, exploration, diffusion, generalization

Feedback

Use a signal after an attempt to change what the system does next time.

Why it exists: A learner cannot improve unless something tells it how its guesses or actions worked out.

Concepts: transformer block, pretraining, fine tuning, agents and tools, evaluation, reward, policy gradient, actor critic, offline rl, rl for llms, guidance, scaling laws

Scale

Ask what changes when data, model size, compute, or test-time work becomes much larger.

Why it exists: Some behavior is invisible at small size because the system has too little capacity or practice.

Concepts: pretraining, rl for llms, scaling laws

Invariance

Preserve the meaning while surface details such as position, wording, or style change.

Why it exists: The same idea can appear in many forms, and a useful model should not relearn it from scratch every time.

Concepts: positional encoding, vision transformers, generalization

Composition

Build complex behavior by connecting reusable smaller operations.

Why it exists: Large tasks are too varied to solve as one giant special case.

Concepts: transformer block, reasoning traces, agents and tools, vision transformers
